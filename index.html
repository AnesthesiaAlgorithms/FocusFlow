Focused Preoperative Cardiac Ultrasound (FoCUS) Training

This brief module is designed to introduce certified registered nurse anesthetists (CRNAs), student registered nurse anesthetists (SRNAs), residents, and faculty to the basic principles of focused preoperative transthoracic echocardiography (FoCUS).

You will complete a short knowledge and confidence questionnaire before and after working through the training content. The entire module takes approximately 30–45 minutes.

What to expect

1. A brief demographics questionnaire
2. A pre-training knowledge test, confidence survey, and case scenarios
3. Five short training modules covering FoCUS views, red-flag findings, and decision-making
4. An optional hands-on "virtual probe" simulation using your phone
5. A post-training knowledge test, confidence survey, case scenarios, and brief feedback survey

About your responses

Participation is voluntary and anonymous. No patient information is collected or entered at any point.
